Questions.

Can I give from a donor-advised fund?

Yes. Log in to your fund (Fidelity Charitable, Schwab, Vanguard, or any other) and recommend a grant to Axiom Church in Leander, TX. EIN 99-2996628. Most funds already have us on file, so search by name or EIN. Add your name and email to the grant so we know who to thank. Grants usually arrive in one to three weeks.

Is my gift tax-deductible?

Axiom Church is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so gifts are tax-deductible as the law allows. Log in to the giving form above any time to download your statement.

Questions about giving?

Call 512-265-5718 or send us a message. A real person will get back to you.
