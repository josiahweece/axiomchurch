Why we started

Most people don’t have a problem with Jesus.

They have a problem with people misrepresenting him.

I’ve watched friends I love walk into church and feel it in about thirty seconds. Wrong clothes. Too many tattoos. Too many questions nobody wanted.

So Jessica and I started Axiom to clear the way.

What Axiom is

A church for people who thought they’d never step foot in one.

Our mission is to remove every obstacle so that people can get to Jesus.

Axiom Church is a non-denominational church in Leander, Texas, and a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. We meet Sundays at 10am at Camacho Elementary. Loud music, and a message from the Bible that makes sense even if you’ve never opened one. Axiom Kids has rooms for ages 0 through elementary. People drive in from Leander, Cedar Park, Liberty Hill and North Austin.

After Sunday there’s a clear path. Rally Point is everyone’s first step. Base Camp helps you find where you fit. From there you get baptized, join a team, get in a group and give to the mission. And if you’re in a hard spot, Axiom Cares helps you get the help you need.
