Our mantras

Who we are.

  • This church exists for the one, and we will do anything short of sin to help them meet Jesus.

  • Giving our time, talents, and treasures back to God creates a unique and addictive joy.

  • We have a bias toward action, a comfort with risk, and show up to serve.

  • You were not created to do life alone. We intentionally link ourselves to others.

  • We experience the good life God designed when we allow Jesus to direct how we live.

Our model

We help you

Find friends.

You know your neighbor's dog's name. Not your neighbor's. Making friends as an adult is hard. Let us help.

Meet Jesus.

Most people don't have a problem with Jesus. They have a problem with people misrepresenting him. It is time to meet the real Jesus.

Break the cycle.

If nothing changes, nothing changes. It is time to try something that will actually work.

Make a difference.

A good life isn't the same as a life that matters. Everybody wants their life to count. Come find the more you were created for.

Why we started

“This place isn't made for me.”

I've watched friends I love walk into church and feel it in about thirty seconds.

Wrong clothes. Too many tattoos. Too many questions nobody wanted.

I wanted them to meet Jesus. Too much got in the way.

That's why Jessica and I started Axiom. To clear the way.

It's what I wanted for my friends. It's what I want for you.

Josiah WeeceLead Pastor
