What kind of church is this?
Non-denominational. Casual. We teach from the Bible every week, but we’re not afraid of your questions.
Is it far from Cedar Park or Round Rock?
Not really. Most people drive in from Leander, Cedar Park, Liberty Hill, Round Rock, Georgetown and North Austin. Cedar Park and Liberty Hill are about 15 minutes. Round Rock, Georgetown and North Austin are about 25.
What is the time commitment?
About 65 minutes. A few songs, a Bible teaching you can use on Tuesday.
What should I wear?
Wear what you would wear on Saturday. No dress code. Just please wear something.
Can I just sit in the back?
Yes. Nobody gets asked to stand up, raise a hand, or introduce themselves.
What if church hurt me?
If church hurt you, we’re sorry. You don’t have to trust us yet. Sit in the back. Leave early. Come back if you want to.
I’m LGBTQ+. Am I welcome?
Everyone is welcome. This is a place you can belong before you believe the same things we do. Read what we believe.
Are you going to ask me for money?
No obligation to give as a guest. We're just glad you're here.
What happens after I fill out the form?
One email from a real person before Sunday. If you have kids, they'll be pre-registered. That's it.
What if Axiom isn’t for me?
That’s okay. We would rather you find a church than find ours. Here are a few nearby we’d happily send you to.
