    See the whole list
    1. In one God…

      who is Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

      • John 1:1-14
    2. God is the Creator…

      of all things.

      • Genesis 1:1
    3. In Jesus Christ…

      God’s only Son and Savior of the world.

      • John 3:16
    4. In the Holy Spirit…

      who encourages, guides, comforts, and transforms in the way of Christ.

      • Galatians 5:22,23
    5. The Bible…

      is the inspired written Word of God and the authoritative rule for all matters of faith and practice.

      • 2 Timothy 3:16-17
    6. Mankind…

      created by God, willfully sinned, and as a result, is lost and without hope apart from Jesus Christ, whose death, burial, and resurrection, opened the way for salvation.

      • Romans 3:23,24
    7. Our sin…

      separates us from God and leaves us hopeless. Sin does not just go against what God says, it goes against who God is.

      • Romans 3:23
      • 1 John 1:5,6
    8. Salvation…

      is the forgiveness of sins—comes by grace alone through the blood of Jesus Christ. It is offered to all who believe and put their faith in Jesus Christ.

      • Ephesians 2:8
    9. The Bible clearly teaches…

      that all believers need to confess and repent of sin, follow the biblical command to be immersed, and strive to daily live faithfully committed to Christ.

      • Acts 2:38
    10. Death…

      seals our eternal destiny. The saved receive eternal life; the unsaved face eternal separation from God.

      • Romans 8:1-12
      • Romans 10:9
      • John 10:10
      • Proverbs 3:5,6
    11. The Church…

      is the Body and Bride of Christ. It began on Pentecost and includes all Christians everywhere.

      • Acts 2
    12. Sex…

      was designed for marriage between a man and a woman.

      • Genesis 2:24
      • Matthew 19:4-6
      • Hebrews 13:4
    13. Danger…

      is guaranteed. When you follow Jesus, you are not kissing your hardships goodbye. You gain heaven as an ally, but hell as an enemy. But you won’t be alone. Jesus doesn’t say “I will overcome.” He says “I have.”

      • Matthew 10:16
      • John 16:33
    14. You…

      are welcome at Axiom Church, regardless of your hurts, habits, or hang-ups. No one will be barred from the love or grace of God here. At the same time, as a church we will never affirm nor celebrate sin. We love you enough to say come as you are, but we love you too much to let you stay as you are!
