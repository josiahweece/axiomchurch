What we believe

Got questions?

Just ask. Honest questions are welcome here.

01

One God

Who is Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. (John 1:1–14)

02

God is the Creator

Of all things. (Genesis 1:1)

03

Jesus Christ

God’s only Son and Savior of the world. (John 3:16)

04

The Holy Spirit

Encourages, guides, comforts, and transforms in the way of Christ. (Galatians 5:22–23)

05

The Bible

The inspired written Word of God and authoritative rule for faith and practice. (2 Timothy 3:16–17)

06

Mankind and sin

Created by God, willfully sinned, and without hope apart from Jesus Christ. Sin separates us from God. (Romans 3:23–24)

07

Salvation

Forgiveness comes by grace through Jesus Christ and is offered to all who put their faith in him. (Ephesians 2:8)

08

Following Jesus

Believers confess and repent, follow the command to be immersed, and live faithfully committed to Christ. (Acts 2:38)

09

Eternity

Death seals our eternal destiny. The saved receive eternal life; the unsaved face eternal separation from God.

10

The Church

The Body and Bride of Christ, begun at Pentecost and including Christians everywhere. (Acts 2)

11

Sex and marriage

Sex was designed for marriage between a man and a woman. (Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:4–6; Hebrews 13:4)

12

You are welcome

Regardless of your hurts, habits, or hang-ups, no one is barred from God’s love or grace here. Come as you are; we love you too much to let you stay as you are.
